MigFlow

MigFlow is a free and open-source software for immersed granular flows, developed through a collaboration between UCLouvain and the Université de Montpellier.

Immersed granular flows arise in a wide range of scientific and industrial applications, including submarine avalanches and lava flows in geology, granular catalysts in chemistry, pile driving in civil engineering, crop mixing in agri-food engineering, and powder transport in the pharmaceutical industry.

The project aims to simulate grains in a fluid in complex configurations while maintaining an efficient computational cost.
