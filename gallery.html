Gallery

A selection of movies from my research on granular flows, free-surface dynamics, and coupled multiphysics simulations.

Beautiful simulations are often a strong motivation to keep working on a problem, and I hope these visualizations can inspire others as well.

Some of them are work in progress and should be taken with a grain of salt, but I find them visually appealing nonetheless.
