Michel Henry

I am a researcher in the MigFlow team of the Applied Mechanics and Mathematics division of the IMMC Institute at UCLouvain.

My research focuses on numerical methods for coupled granular-fluid flows and free-surface dynamics. I work on PFEM, DEM/FEM coupling, adaptive remeshing, and scientific software development, with applications to immersed granular collapse, landslides, and multiphase systems.

Keywords: Computational mechanics, granular-fluid interactions, Particle Finite Element Method, non-smooth contact dynamics.

Contact: michel.henry1996@gmail.com

Research Interests

Immersed Granular Flows

Numerical modelling of granular-fluid interactions, collapse dynamics, and free-surface phenomena.

Particle Finite Element Method

Adaptive remeshing, sharp-interface methods, and multiphysics simulations for complex free-surface flows.

Non-Smooth Contact Dynamics

Contact mechanics, frictional systems, and large-scale DEM/FEM coupling for particulate materials.
