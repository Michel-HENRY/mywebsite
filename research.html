Research Interests

Immersed Granular Flows

Numerical modelling of granular-fluid interactions, collapse dynamics, and free-surface phenomena.

Particle Finite Element Method

Adaptive remeshing, sharp-interface methods, and multiphysics simulations for complex free-surface flows.

Non-Smooth Contact Dynamics

Contact mechanics, frictional systems, and large-scale DEM/FEM coupling for particulate materials.
